## Corrections

- **Passage:** removed the invented door, header and lintel across M2's central passage and the invisible movement boundary. Restored the view toward the centered rear door and opened both storeroom leaves to their reviewed M2 poses. Both storerooms are reachable; their detail remains blockout-level and the rear door remains closed.
- **Pane divisions:** reference G01 shows two columns and five rows in the tall recessed door glazing. Replaced the short, coarse cross with a continuous grid and room-facing glazing stops, correctly mirrored between the leaves. The angled return windows retain the approved single-pane arrangement and 15° tilt. Glass appearance is unchanged.
- **Inside trim:** connected display-window reveals, jambs, head casings, sills and aprons; added angled return casings and slender moving sash stops.
- **Age and details:** added separate faded interior paint/wood materials, local chipped edges, small plaster paint losses and hairline cracks. Added drawer faces/pulls, shelf fasteners and faded label holders, old outlets and conduit/clips, a stopped clock and retained faded notices. These details are invented. There is no loose debris or overgrowth.
- **Front:** retained the shell, brick treatment, main display windows, canopy and apron. The corrected recessed door grid/hardware and interior seen through the glazing are the visible changes.

These are allocations, not proof that the complete scene will meet its frame-time target.

Consider KTX2/mesh compression when higher-resolution materials or site assets require it. Retain one shadowed daylight source and re-profile substantial additions.
