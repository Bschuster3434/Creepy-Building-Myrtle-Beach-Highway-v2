#### M3 — Small playable quality sample
**Outcome:** A small browser experience that establishes the visual and technical standard for completing the building.
**Recommended sample:** The complete representative front sales room and adjoining entrance, display glazing, canopy and a limited apron/side-return area. The rest of the building remains blockout context. If M2 yields one large undivided sales room, agree its extent before detailing; a decorated corner alone does not satisfy the furnished-room milestone.
**Proposed work:**
1. Finish the sample with convincing close-range brick/mortar, frames, glazing, floor, ceiling, trim and realistic daytime lighting. Carry the long-unused mood through restrained fading, worn finishes and sparse remaining furnishings. Detail the empty front chair as a focal point, with proposed empty shelving, a retained counter and a few empty crates. Leave displays unstocked so the store reads as closed. Keep surfaces clean and vegetation controlled; dirt, debris and overgrowth are outside Brian’s requested treatment. These furnishing and finish choices are proposed interpretations of his preference.
2. Establish a reproducible Blender-to-GLB export and V2 React/Three.js application. Preserve interactive parts, pivots, material scale and collision representations; record asset provenance and export settings.
3. Implement first-person movement and collision plus click operation of a representative door, the agreed window and a light switch controlling an actual light. Check closed/open door passage, window states, repeated operation, thresholds and recovery from lost pointer focus.
4. Inspect a repeatable route from apron through entrance and around the furnished room, with close-ups of materials, props and window/door details. Review the browser result with Brian and record the accepted visual benchmark.
5. Select target hardware, browsers, viewport and input support at M3 kickoff. Desktop keyboard/mouse is the proposed starting baseline; mobile support remains a decision. Agree numeric frame-time, loading/download and scene-resource budgets, then measure cold loading and a repeatable walking route under recorded conditions.
6. Record sample triangles, draw calls, textures/memory estimates and lighting cost; estimate the completed building/site and reserve capacity for vegetation and remaining rooms. Identify required reuse, instancing, compression or lighting changes before extending production.
**Deliverables:** Updated Blender source, exported sample assets, runnable V2 application with setup instructions, visual review captures, interaction checklist, measured performance report and full-scene budget plan.
**Complete when:** The browser sample meets the agreed visual benchmark, all three representative interactions and movement checks pass, and measured results meet the agreed budgets with a credible allowance for the full environment. A smooth sample alone is insufficient evidence for full-scene performance.
**Boundary:** Full building/environment production remains M4; all doors/windows/lights and finished orbit/top-down modes remain M5; public release remains M6. Day/night remains optional.
