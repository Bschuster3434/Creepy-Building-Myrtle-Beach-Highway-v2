# M3 produce service shop — revision 3

**Visual benchmark approved.** Brian accepted revision 3's layout and atmosphere with “Nope, that feels totally right.” M3 remains In Progress for target-device/budget confirmation only. He likes the building and general interior, but felt the front room was too deep and lacked a specific identity. He described a produce shop where people were served, with customers occupying the first 5–10 feet, and a quiet, sad atmosphere. How much stock was left behind was undecided; this revision uses mostly empty fixtures and a few retained tools.

## Layout and appearance

- The service counter's customer edge sits 2.40 m from the facade, approximately 6.9 feet inside the recessed doors. Its 5.30 m top spans most of the room, with a short return on the right. The left end leaves a 1.28 m access opening.
- Storeroom fronts move from 8.80 to 5.80 m, reducing the front room from roughly 29 to 19 feet. The opposing storeroom door centers move from 9.85 to 6.85 m. Passage walls and skirting extend back to the same rear endpoint; the central passage stays open.
- The empty chair remains behind the counter on the right. Drawers, cupboards, worn painted panels and pulls make the counter read as a permanent service fixture.
- Three empty slatted produce bins, a mechanical weighing scale with dial and metal pan, a few flattened paper bags and a pencil establish a former serving area. A faded produce price board adds a specific use. These props, prices and their placement are invented.
- Pendant positions follow the new room arrangement. Existing aged finishes, stopped clock, empty shelves and notices remain. Review captures include both daylight and switched lights.
- Exterior geometry, glass and the corrected recessed window/door trim remain as reviewed. The M2 source and all V1 assets are unchanged. This M3 interior revision supersedes M2's provisional front-room depth for the sample.

Collision testing caught a crowded approach beside the produce bench; its final position leaves the route clear.

The 22 generated 512² material images retain the conservative 29.33 MiB RGBA+mipmap estimate; 20 are used in exported materials. The renderer reports 21 textures including the shadow map. Sampled draw submissions remain below the provisional 150-call cap, including shadow passes.

The Vite Three.js chunk-size advisory remains; measured loading meets the proposed sample budget. Frame intervals include browser scheduling rather than isolated GPU time. Desktop device and numeric budgets remain unconfirmed, and these results do not establish complete-scene performance.

Retain the provisional full-scene planning ceilings of 750k unique triangles, 350 draw submissions and 256 MiB material textures. Re-profile substantial later additions; rear-room dressing and the wider site are still unfinished.
